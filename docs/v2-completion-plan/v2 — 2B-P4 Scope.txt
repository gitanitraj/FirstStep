# First Step v2 — 2B-P4 Scope

## 1. Purpose

2B-P4 defines the implementation boundary for completing First Step v2.

It translates the approved:

- seven resident/editorial journeys;
- five Epics;
- Completion Contract;
- reconciliation matrix;
- and closure backlog

into a finite scope classification.

Every remaining item must be classified as one of:

### V2 Required

The item is directly necessary to satisfy a contracted resident/editorial journey or release acceptance condition.

If it is not completed, v2 cannot close.

### V2 Enabling Foundation

The item is not itself a resident journey, but a narrowly bounded supporting change is necessary to make a required v2 behavior reliable.

Only the minimum needed foundation belongs in v2.

### V3 / Later

The item may be useful, desirable, or strategically important, but it is not necessary to satisfy the Completion Contract.

Its absence does not block v2.

---

# 2. Scope Rule

After approval of this scope:

**Newly discovered work does not automatically enter v2.**

It enters only when:

1. it is necessary to satisfy an existing v2 requirement;
2. it is the minimum enabling foundation required by that requirement; or
3. the Completion Contract and this scope are explicitly amended.

Technical elegance, future usefulness, historical aspiration, or partial implementation are not sufficient reasons to expand v2.

---

# 3. Epic 1 — Civic Catalog Identity & Relationships

## V2 Required

### P4-1. Preserve offering / organization / producer distinctions

First Step must continue to distinguish:

- the civic offering;
- the organization associated with it;
- the content producer/source;
- and the provenance through which First Step received the information.

No completion work may collapse those identities.

**Supports:** J3, J5, J7

---

### P4-2. Preserve community as explicit scope

`communityId` or equivalent community ownership remains first-class.

Remaining v2 work must not introduce Wilmington as an implicit universal constant.

**Supports:** J7 and multi-community readiness

---

### P4-3. Maintain resident-meaningful classification

Category, subcategory/topic, notice kind, content source, organization, and resident-facing collections must remain distinct concepts.

Internal classifications must not leak directly into resident navigation.

**Supports:** J1, J2, J4, J7

---

## V2 Enabling Foundation

### P4-4. Targeted model changes required by remaining stories

Small model changes are allowed only where required for:

- expiry eligibility;
- approaching-expiry detection;
- Expert/FAQ category integrity;
- deterministic featured selection;
- Community Event generalization.

This does not authorize a broad domain remodel.

---

## V3 / Later

- sophisticated organization profiles;
- Institution/Party cross-role identity;
- generalized relationship graph;
- Community administration system;
- second-community launch;
- organization history/alias system beyond existing needs.

---

# 4. Epic 2 — Resident Help & Topic Discovery

## V2 Required

### P4-5. Complete Category Destination

The category destination must integrate:

- featured topics;
- Get Help;
- What to Know;
- Organizations;
- accepted context rail behavior;
- deeper collection paths.

**Supports:** J1, J2, J4

---

### P4-6. Topic sub-destination

A resident can select a topic and reach a deeper collection of relevant content.

The experience must retain category context and avoid exposing raw taxonomy.

**Supports:** J2

---

### P4-7. All-category help path

Residents must be able to browse all eligible help in a category without choosing a featured topic first.

Topics are discovery aids, not gates.

**Supports:** J1

---

### P4-8. Featured topic selection

Featured topics are selected from the strongest eligible category inventory.

Expired content must not inflate topic counts.

Selection must be deterministic.

**Supports:** J2

---

### P4-9. Role-based featured item selection

Each featured topic may show up to three role-based positions:

1. **Timely / Actionable**
2. **Durable Help**
3. **Visual / Additional Discovery**

Where multiple candidates satisfy a role, selection follows deterministic metadata-based tie-breaking.

No universal relevance-score engine is required.

**Supports:** J2

---

### P4-10. Direct source/content navigation

Featured/topic content should take the resident directly to the relevant source or resident-facing content destination.

**Supports:** J1, J2

---

## V2 Enabling Foundation

### P4-11. Deterministic selection utility/service

A narrowly bounded selector may be introduced to:

- calculate eligible topic inventory;
- assign candidates to featured roles;
- apply stable tie-breaking.

It must not become a generalized recommendation engine.

---

### P4-12. Eligible-content projection

Get Help and topic collections need a reusable way to receive only resident-eligible content.

This should build on the expiry rule rather than duplicate it.

---

## V3 / Later

- editorial override UI for featured topics;
- recommendation engine;
- personalization;
- demographic recommendation system;
- automatic ranking based on engagement;
- AI-generated content ranking.

---

# 5. Epic 3 — Civic Information Collections

## V2 Required

### P4-13. Preserve controlled What to Know collections

For Housing:

- Policy Info;
- Law;
- Flyers;
- First Rate News.

These remain controlled resident-facing projections.

**Supports:** J4

---

### P4-14. Flyers / Get Help overlap rule

A flyer may appear in Flyers and also in Get Help when the underlying opportunity genuinely represents access to help.

No duplicate underlying record is required.

**Supports:** J1, J4

---

### P4-15. Expert / FAQ category integrity

Expert and FAQ content expected to participate in category navigation must have valid category relationships.

Missing or invalid classification must be detectable.

**Supports:** J4, J7

---

### P4-16. First Step editorial content remains admissible

Approved First Step Originals, Expert material, and appropriate FAQ content must participate in category navigation according to the accepted First Rate News definition.

**Supports:** J4

---

### P4-17. Community Events generalization

The architecture must represent community events without treating them as Housing-style resources.

The United Neighbors 2026 example is an accepted test candidate:

- one underlying civic item;
- notice kind = Event;
- flyer media retained;
- Community Notices → Events;
- Community Notices → Flyers;
- temporal eligibility after event conclusion;
- no new `ContentType`.

**Supports:** J7

---

## V2 Enabling Foundation

### P4-18. Controlled projection rules

Small shared projection rules may be introduced for:

- Events;
- Flyers;
- Get Help;
- First Rate News;
- Policy Info.

The backend must not auto-enumerate arbitrary values.

---

### P4-19. Human-origin editorial provenance

Expert and FAQ content should preserve whether the material is:

- First Step-created;
- First Step-initiated;
- based on an interview;
- rewritten from source material;
- or otherwise attributable.

This does not require a generalized editorial provenance platform.

---

## V3 / Later

- automatic FAQ import/publication;
- generalized external-content republishing;
- automated interview transcription pipeline;
- content syndication system;
- generalized event platform.

---

# 6. Epic 4 — Content Lifecycle & Editorial Stewardship

## V2 Required

### P4-20. Expiry eligibility

Content must resolve into:

- Active;
- Approaching Expiry;
- Expired.

Resident eligibility rules must enforce those states where expiry is meaningful.

**Supports:** J6

---

### P4-21. Expired-content exclusion

Expired material must be excluded from resident-facing surfaces that imply current availability.

Retention for editorial/history is allowed.

**Supports:** J6

---

### P4-22. Approaching-expiry visibility

Admins/editors need a repeatable mechanism to identify approaching-expiry records.

For v2, a validator/report/diagnostic is sufficient.

**Supports:** J6

---

### P4-23. Admin expiry guidance

The separate Admin-facing expiry document becomes part of v2 operational documentation.

It defines:

- Active;
- Approaching Expiry;
- Expired;
- editorial review expectations;
- role-based featured behavior.

**Supports:** J6 and documentation readiness

---

### P4-24. Public/private editorial boundaries

Review state, generation provenance, flag disposition, and other internal editorial fields remain excluded from public DTOs where already established.

**Supports:** J4 and quality gates

---

## V2 Enabling Foundation

### P4-25. Shared eligibility function

A single reusable eligibility rule should prevent Category, Search, Get Help, Events, and other projections from independently inventing expiry behavior.

This should remain narrow.

---

### P4-26. Expiry diagnostics

A report/validator may expose:

- record ID;
- title;
- expiry date;
- current expiry state;
- content class;
- enough identifying metadata for editorial review.

---

## V3 / Later — Editorial Operations & Stewardship

The following are explicitly deferred:

- Admin dashboard;
- automated editorial queues;
- workflow assignment;
- email notifications;
- automatic article creation;
- editorial task routing;
- editorial follow-up status;
- richer lifecycle management.

These should build on the v2 eligibility semantics rather than replace them.

---

# 7. Epic 5 — Catalog Integrity & Classification

## V2 Required

### P4-27. Validate required category relationships

Expert/FAQ and other contracted content must not disappear because required category relationships are missing.

---

### P4-28. Validate organization/source identity

Known invalid or missing references that can damage resident journeys must be caught.

---

### P4-29. Stable featured selection

Given the same eligible catalog, featured-topic and featured-item results must be reproducible.

No array-order dependency.

---

### P4-30. Protect fixed ContentType boundary

Remaining v2 work must not add a new `ContentType` merely to represent:

- Events;
- FAQs;
- Originals;
- resident collections;
- discovery concepts.

---

### P4-31. Validate controlled collection rules

Resident-facing collections must follow explicit projection rules rather than arbitrary internal enumeration.

---

## V2 Enabling Foundation

Additional validator rules are permitted when they protect:

- expiry;
- category integrity;
- organization/source identity;
- deterministic selection;
- controlled projections.

No generalized schema-governance framework is required.

# 10. P4 Scope Summary

## Actual v2 build work

The remaining substantive implementation is:

1. expiry eligibility and exclusion;
2. approaching-expiry reporting;
3. Expert/FAQ category integrity;
4. topic/Get Help collection behavior;
5. deterministic featured-topic selection;
6. role-based featured-item selection;
7. final Category Destination integration;
8. any small model/validator changes strictly necessary to support those behaviors.

## V2 acceptance work

Then:

1. Community Events generalization test;
2. provenance audit;
3. multi-community audit;
4. seven-journey regression;
5. accessibility/responsive verification;
6. automated test/validator pass;
7. documentation reconciliation.

## Not v2

Everything else remains outside the release unless the Completion Contract is explicitly amended.

---

# 11. P4 Exit Criterion

2B-P4 is complete when:

- every remaining story is classified;
- no story is simultaneously treated as v2 and future work;
- each V2 Enabling Foundation item is bounded by a named resident/editorial requirement;
- V3/Later work is explicitly recorded;
- the remaining v2 build list is finite;
- the scope is approved before logical ER/classification design begins.

Once approved, **2B-P5 may model only what this scope authorizes.**
